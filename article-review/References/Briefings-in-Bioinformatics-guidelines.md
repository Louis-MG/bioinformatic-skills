## Journal Overview

### About Briefings in Bioinformatics

*Briefings in Bioinformatics* (Online ISSN: 1477-4054) is a peer-reviewed, **fully open access** journal published by Oxford University Press. The journal provides an indispensable resource for experimental practitioners seeking awareness of data sources and analytical tools in contemporary biology, biotechnology, and medicine at the **molecular level**.

### Journal Aims

The journal aims to:
- Provide awareness of disparate sources of data and analytical tools in molecular biology
- Cover all areas of **omics** sciences: genomics, proteomics, lipidomics, glycomics, metabolomics, interactomics, network biology, imaging, systems biology, phenomics, chemoinformatics, computational biology, and clinical/medical informatics
- Address the shift from laboratory time to **data analysis and interpretation** as the rate-limiting step
- Provide **topical reviews** of new methodologies as they become established
- Include reviews of education and training in biomedical sciences

### Editorial Focus

The journal welcomes submissions that:
- Cover **clearly defined subject areas** for both experimental biologists and bioinformatics specialists
- Address **variety of approaches** to well-specified biological problems
- Explain **underlying concepts** and selection of correct tools
- Discuss **limitations** and **interpretation of results**
- Provide **transparent, user-friendly** descriptions of analytical solutions

**Methodological Approaches of Interest:**
- Software comparison and benchmarking
- Data cleaning and curation
- Accuracy of predicted and extracted information
- Ontologies and text-mining
- High-performance computing solutions and cloud systems
- Standards, training, and change management activities
- Determination of causal relationships from data
- Ontologies for semantic-based analysis of molecular data
- Methods and tools for automatic/semi-automatic annotation
- Methods and tools for enrichment analysis
- **Replicability and reproducibility** of bioinformatics methodologies
- **Independent evaluation** of software tools for common tasks

**Important Note**: The journal is a **review journal** and does **not** publish articles that are pure original research. It focuses on methodological reviews, comparisons, and evaluations.

## Article Types and Requirements

### Article Types Accepted

| Article Type | Description | Word Limit | Key Requirements |
|--------------|-------------|------------|------------------|
| **Reviews** | Surveys of previously published research in specified areas, giving overview and recommendations for optimal problem-solving approaches | 2,000-7,000 words | Do NOT include new results from your own work; must cover variety of approaches; provide recommendations |
| **Problem Solving Protocols** | Protocols for solving specific problems using different sets of programs; demonstration of new methodological application to meaningful biological problem | 2,000-5,000 words | Real experimental data must be analyzed; demonstrate novel application; original algorithms also accepted |
| **Case Studies** | Novel applications of methods, pipelines, or software to specific bioinformatics problem | 2,000-5,000 words | Applied to biological research or clinical practice; significant novelty in analysis of real experimental data |
| **Position Articles** | Discuss specific bioinformatics topic/issue from position of authority | 2,000-7,000 words | **By invitation only**; clear viewpoint, background info, evidence, opposing views, recommendations; unsolicited submissions not accepted |
| **Opinion Articles** | Topical or controversial areas not warranting full review | 500-1,000 words | Focused commentary on emerging issues |
| **Letters to the Editors** | Critique of previously published article in the journal | 500-1,000 words | Constructive, professional, polite criticism |
| **Software and Website Reviews** | Reviews of bioinformatics software/websites | 500-1,000 words | **Must be written by authors OTHER than originators** |
| **Book Reviews** | Book reviews | 500-1,000 words | Standard review format |

### Important Notes on Scope

- **NOT a research journal**: Briefings in Bioinformatics does **not** publish pure original research
- **Focus**: Methodological reviews, evaluations, comparisons
- **Strength**: Independent evaluation of software tools by non-originators
- **Encouraged**: Papers comparing multiple tools/parameter settings for given problems

## Formatting Guidelines

### General Formatting

**Language:**
- Manuscripts must be **clearly written in English**
- Avoid language or slang not keeping with academic and professional style
- Do NOT use journal as vehicle for marketing specific products/services

**Style Manual:**
- Follow conventions of **CSE Style Manual** (Council of Science Editors, Reston, VA, 2006)

**Specialized Terminology:**
- **Chemical names**: Follow Chemical Abstracts and its indices
- **Biochemical terminology**: Follow IUPAC-IUB Commission on Biochemical Nomenclature (1992, Biochemical Society, UK)
- **Enzymes**: Use recommended names from IUPAC-IUB Committee (1978, Enzyme Nomenclature, Academic Press)
- **Units**: Use SI units wherever possible
- **Genotypes**: Italicized
- **Phenotypes**: NOT italicized
- **Bacterial agents**: Follow Demerec et al. (1966) Genetics 54, 61-76 and Trends In Genetics Nomenclature Guide (1998), Elsevier, Cambridge
- **Organization names**: Write out in full first, followed by initials in brackets, then use initials only
  - Example: "Food and Drug Administration (FDA)" first use, then "FDA"

**Format-Free Submission:**
- Manuscripts can be submitted **without formatting** to journal style
- Will need to be formatted for revision after acceptance

### Title Page

**Required Elements:**
1. **Title**
2. **Author names** (full names)
3. **Authors' institutional affiliations**
4. **Corresponding author contact information:**
   - Telephone number
   - Fax number
   - Email address

**Author Description:**
- All papers should be accompanied by short description of author(s) (~30 words)
- If appropriate, include organization affiliation

### Abstracts

**Requirement:**
- All review articles and software/website reviews require abstract
- **Purpose**: Outline aims and subject matter
- **Key Points**: Up to **5 key points** (see below)
- **Keywords**: Up to **6 keywords** for indexing purposes

### Key Points

**Location**: Displayed at end of article

**Requirements:**
- **Number**: 3-5 brief sentences
- **Purpose**: Summarize key messages of article
- **Content**: Can include anything important for readers to note

### References

**Citation Style:**
- Follow **Oxford SCIMED** style
- [Oxford SCIMED Requirements](https://static.primary.prod.gcms.the-infra.com/static/site/bioinformatics/document/Ministylechecklist_Oxford_SCIMED_A-D.docx)

**General Rules:**
- All references **must be cited in text**
- Denote using **numbers in square brackets** before punctuation
  - Example: [1, 3-5]
- References **numbered consecutively** in order of appearance

**Reference Style Examples:**

1. Journal Article:
   ```
   Attwood T.K. The role of pattern databases in sequence analysis. Brief Bioinform 2000;1:45-59.
   ```

2. Book:
   ```
   Long HC, Blatt MA, Higgins MC et al. Medical Decision Making. Boston: Butterworth-Heinemann, 1997.
   ```

3. Book Chapter:
   ```
   Manners T, Jones R, Riley M. Relationship of overweight to haitus hernia and reflux oesophagitis. In: Newman W (ed). The Obesity Conundrum. Amsterdam: Elsevier Science, 1997, 352-74.
   ```

4. Conference Abstract:
   ```
   Hou Y, Qiu Y, Vo NH et al. 23-O derivatives of OMT: highly active against H. influenzae. In: Programs and Abstracts of the Forty-third Interscience Conference on Antimicrobial Agents and Chemotherapy, Chicago, IL, 2003. Abstract F-1187, p.242. American Society for Microbiology, Washington, DC, USA.
   ```

5. Website:
   ```
   Public Health Laboratory Service. Antimicrobial Resistance in 2000: England and Wales. http://www.hpa.org.uk./infections/topics_az/antimicrobial_resistance/amr.pdf (7 January 2004, date last accessed).
   ```

**Additional Formatting:**
- **4+ authors**: Use first three followed by "et al."
- **Papers in preparation**: Should NOT be in reference list
- **Papers submitted for publication**: Should NOT be in reference list
- **Accuracy**: Ensure references to named people/organizations are accurate and without libellous implications
- **Web Links**: Include date last accessed in parentheses

### Figures

**General Requirements:**
- Should be **referred to in text** and **numbered consecutively**
- **Submission**: Separate from main text
- **Position**: Indicate approximate final positions in text
- **Legends**: Marked within main text
- **Legends Content**: Should describe figure content and be understandable independently from text
- **Abbreviations**: Avoid in figures; if used, explain in legend or key

**Technical Specifications:**
- **Format**: Electronic format suitable for printing
- **Resolution**:
  - **Line drawings and combinations**: 600 dpi
  - **Greyscale**: 300 dpi
- **Quality**: High quality required for publication
- **Color**: Online color figures are **free of charge**

**Chart Guidelines:**
- **Line charts, bar charts, pie charts**: Should be two-dimensional
- **Background**: Single categories, generous margin, grey-scaled backgrounds (25% tint)
- **Scales**: Appropriate scales should be used; sources quoted
- **Bar charts**: Must have ≥2 categories and ≥5 observations (otherwise present as table)
- **Horizontal lines**: Use to mark major values on y-axis
- **Line charts**: Should show changes over long time spans with ≥10 observations
- **Pie charts**: Show proportions; minimum 4 segments, maximum 12 segments

### Photographs

**Requirements:**
- Can be supplied as good quality black and white or color
- Must have sufficient detail, contrast, and fineness of grain
- **Resolution**: Minimum 300 dpi
- **Format**: GIF, TIFF, or BITMAP files preferred for electronic copies
- **Position**: Indicate approximate final positions in text
- **Note**: Photos should withstand loss of contrast inherent in printing process

### Internet Screen Dumps

**Requirements:**
- Must be **greyscale** with **white background** (increases contrast)
- **Format**: BITMAP
- **Resolution**: Minimum 300 dpi
- **Position**: Indicate approximate final positions in margin of text
- **Warning**: Low resolution graphics may not reproduce well; avoid when possible

### Tables

**Submission:**
- Electronic form preferred (MS Word or Excel)
- **Referred to in text** and **numbered consecutively**
- **Position**: Indicate approximate final positions in text

**Formatting:**
- Each column should have **short heading**
- **Units**: State where appropriate in column headings
- **Legends**: Should describe content and be understandable independently from text
- **Alignment**: Data columns should be right-hand aligned or aligned by decimal place
- **Sorting**: Data should be sorted where possible
- **Footnotes**: Include on same page as tables
  - Use to explain abbreviations in table
  - Denote by letter
  - Use to quote sources
